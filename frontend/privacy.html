Privacy

BerlinBite sends your restaurant search to Google Maps Platform. Restaurant information is fetched on demand. Saved lists store only place identifiers in your browser; clear your browser storage to remove them.

Optional AI advice sends only a generic time-based crowd estimate to OpenAI, not your restaurant search or Google reviews. Open-Meteo receives restaurant coordinates to return current weather. Our server and hosting provider may process IP addresses and operational request logs for security and reliability.

No advertising analytics or user accounts are included. API keys remain on the server. Do not enter personal or sensitive information in restaurant searches.

See Google Privacy Policy, OpenAI Privacy Policy, and Open-Meteo terms.

Prototype notice: before public deployment, the operator must add their contact details, applicable legal basis, retention periods, and any other required disclosures.
