Terms

BerlinBite is a restaurant discovery prototype. Google Maps information can change or be incomplete. Confirm opening times, language support and availability directly with the restaurant.

Crowd estimates are unvalidated rules based on the time of day and day of week, not measured occupancy or a trained prediction model. AI advice is general guidance and may be inaccurate. BerlinBite does not make reservations.

Use of Google Maps content is subject to the Google Terms of Service and Google Maps Additional Terms. Do not scrape, bulk export or misuse the service.
